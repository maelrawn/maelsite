##### Background

Article 101, Section 3 of the UN Charter states the UN must "[recruit] the staff on as wide a geographical basis as possible".
This has been interpreted to mean the UN's staff nationalities must remain in parity with the global nationality distribution.

This represents a unique issue for recruiting from very small Member States. We call these Member States, UURMS, or Un- and Under-Represented Member States.
The UN's recruitment standards are exceptionally stringent, and socioeconomic factors typically compound to produce a small or nonexistent pool of qualified candidates within very small Member States.
Staff Selection 2.0 is intended to put a focus on this problem, and create unique paths to employment with the UN for UURMS candidates.

##### Solution

I helped implement an ETL pipeline and dashboard in PowerBI. This dashboard provides an overview of UURMS recruitment targets, funnel volumes, and drill-downs for granular data. Dashboard was presented to leadership and put into production
