##### Background

UNFICYP is the UN's peacekeeping brach in Cyprus. Cyprus, a formerly Greek island, was invaded by Turkey in 1974. Once the battle lines became stable, the island was divided into Greek and Turkish halves, which both still dispute the border. UNFICYP is permanently stationed along this border to prevent conflict escalation.

UNFICYP often relies on paper documents to resolve disputes. Old contracts, land surveys, legal opinions, and UNFICYP policies are often required to deescalate situations which might otherwise become violent. This makes time of the essence, and knowledge management the primary issue.

##### Solution

As part of the ReBoot UNtapped hackathon, our team workshopped an AI-powered CRM and communications solution. It was presented to UNFICYP leadership as part of an internal UN competition.
