##### Background

This was Campus' take-home project as part of their interview process. I was tasked with the following:

- Funnel Performance and Admissions Forecasting
- Lead Quality Analysis & Marketing Optimization
- Build Performance Dashboard for Funnel & Team Performance
- Marketing Attribution & Cost-Efficiency Analysis

##### Solutions

- Supplemented existing data with related socioeconomic indicators
- Income by ZIP code, educational attainment by state, historical voting trends, etc
- Aggregated student data across a variety of features, including retention and marketing ROI
- For each combination of features, found correlation of imputed data and retention outcomes
- Ran grid search to find most suitable set of features to use as predictors for ML model
- Built dashboard and presented overview to stakeholders

##### Outcomes

This got me a job at Campus, where I worked on many other databases, pipelines, and analyses.
